## PlayDrop MVP exports

| Channel | Asset | Export |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube Shorts | Shared vertical short | 1080 x 1920 H.264 MP4, roughly 10 to 15 seconds |
| YouTube | Landscape trailer | 1920 x 1080 H.264 MP4, roughly 20 to 30 seconds |
| YouTube | Trailer thumbnail | 1280 x 720 PNG |
| TikTok | Shared vertical short | 1080 x 1920 H.264 MP4, 30 fps, roughly 9 to 15 seconds |
| Instagram Reels and Stories | Shared vertical short | 1080 x 1920 H.264 MP4, at least 30 fps |
| Instagram Feed | Video | 1080 x 1440 H.264 MP4 |
| Instagram Feed | Static carousel | Five 1080 x 1440 PNG files |
| Instagram Reels | Cover | 420 x 654 PNG |
| Pinterest | Standard-width video Pin | 1000 x 1500 H.264 MP4, roughly 6 to 15 seconds |
| Pinterest | Standard image Pins | Five 1000 x 1500 PNG files |
| X | Landscape trailer | Reuse the 1920 x 1080 YouTube trailer |

Pinterest recommends 2:3, or 1000 x 1500, for standard image ads and recommends 6 to 15 seconds for video ads. Instagram retains photos at their original resolution up to 1080 pixels wide when they are within its supported ratio range, currently through 3:4 portrait. Instagram Reels accept up to 9:16 and require at least 720 pixels and 30 fps. TikTok recommends 9:16 and safe-zone-aware creative. YouTube classifies square or vertical uploads up to three minutes as Shorts, while 16:9 remains the landscape trailer format.

## PlayDrop framing rule

Official platform acceptance does not define PlayDrop's quality bar. PlayDrop never ships:

- non-uniform scaling,
- letterboxing or pillarboxing,
- plain side bars,
- blurred or duplicated side fill,
- a cropped-off headline, touch cue, CTA, score, or game goal.

Use a uniform crop within genuine empty margin. Recompose or recapture when that is not possible.
