# Task Routing

- Any game-building request, including new games, remixes, updates, Tweaks, and Playtest Notes: `create-game`.
- Standalone playtest or QA: `playtest-game`.
- Standalone PlayDrop catalogue search: `discover-assets`.
- Standalone transparent 2D asset, sheet, or pack production: `make-2d-asset-pack`.
- Creator support: `creator-help`.
- Marketing positioning, research, and asset planning: `market-game`.
- Only when explicitly requested, final PlayDrop listing screenshots, promotional screenshots, store images, social carousels, and paid-acquisition stills: `make-marketing-screenshots`. These are optional, fully AI-generated marketing images with one truthful two-to-four-word selling-point message each.
- App previews, listing gameplay footage, trailers, gameplay ads, and social video: `make-marketing-video`. Video is the literal representation of gameplay.
- Complete YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, and X delivery package: `make-social-media-package`.
