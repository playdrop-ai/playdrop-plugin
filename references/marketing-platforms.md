# Marketing Platforms

Use this reference when choosing sizes, durations, and platform notes for marketing screenshots and videos. Treat it as plugin guidance that should be reviewed during major platform releases because social upload rules change.

## Asset Families

| Family | Size | Duration | Primary uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical short | 1080x1920 | 8 to 15 seconds by default | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Instagram Stories, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat Spotlight, Pinterest video pins |
| Landscape short | 1920x1080 | 8 to 15 seconds by default | YouTube, X, Reddit, Facebook groups, LinkedIn posts, web embeds |
| Feed portrait | 1080x1350 | still or short loop | Instagram feed, Facebook feed, LinkedIn feed |
| Square feed | 1080x1080 | still or short loop | Instagram feed, Facebook feed, Reddit images, X images |
| Pinterest pin | 1000x1500 | still or short loop | Pinterest standard pins and idea-style posts |
| PlayDrop listing | 1920x1080 and 1080x1920 sources | 8 to 15 seconds by default | PlayDrop listing media, preview proof, thumbnails |

## Platform Notes

- TikTok, Reels, Stories, Shorts, and Snapchat need action in the first 1 to 2 seconds and readable text with sound off.
- YouTube landscape uploads need a separate thumbnail file. Long-form YouTube is out of scope unless the creator asks for a longer capture.
- X and Reddit need a strong first frame because many users see muted autoplay or static previews.
- Facebook groups benefit from a direct caption that explains the game loop and why people should try it.
- Pinterest needs a cover or pin that reads without motion.
- LinkedIn should use concise creator-facing or builder-facing copy when the audience is developers.

## Cover Versus Thumbnail

- Cover images are selected inside upload flows for short-form platforms such as TikTok, Reels, Stories, Shorts, and Snapchat.
- Thumbnail files are exported separately for surfaces such as YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, X, PlayDrop, and web posts.

## Copy Defaults

- Use one simple hook line on the image or video.
- Put controls, objective, and stakes in the caption or description.
- Avoid promising content that is not visible in the actual capture.
- Reuse the same file across platforms when the aspect ratio and audience match, and document reuse in `MARKETING.md`.
